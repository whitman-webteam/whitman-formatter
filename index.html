CMS Format Cleaner & Link Converter

How to use: Paste your formatted text and click Clean. The app will strip bad formatting, convert internal whitman.edu links into CMS xIDs, and automatically format external links. You can manually customize titles directly within the report table.

🛠️ Under the Hood (Technical Details)
  • DOM Sanitization: Strips Google Docs/Word schema tracking (e.g., docs-internal-guid-), drops all inline styles, classes, and empty formatting nodes, mapping structural weights to valid semantic <strong> and <em> tags.
  • Inline Unwrapping: Optionally strips all block-level wrappers (like paragraphs and lists) to safely allow pasting mid-sentence into existing lists or paragraphs without forcing a hard line break in TinyMCE.
  • Internal Link Remapping: Resolves standard whitman.edu hyperlinks by passing them to a Cloudflare Worker that dynamically parses downstream CMS XML headers, re-writing routes locally to the system's preferred XXXXX.xml structure.
  • Social Wall Bypassing: Intercepts Instagram and LinkedIn profiles, pages, jobs, and reels right before network execution. It uses client-side text parsing to instantaneously assemble ADA-compliant titles (bypassing auth-walls and HTTP 429/999 errors entirely).
  • YouTube Meta Grabbing: Queries YouTube's official structured oembed JSON schema endpoint via proxy to automatically fetch accurate media titles.
Turn off if you're pasting into an existing paragraph or list.
