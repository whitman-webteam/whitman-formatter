CMS Format Cleaner & Link Converter

How to use: Paste your formatted text and click Clean. The app will strip bad formatting, convert internal whitman.edu links into CMS xIDs, and automatically format external links to open securely in a new tab with accessible page titles.

Under the Hood (What this tool actually does)
  • External Title Grabbing: The tool automatically reaches out to external websites to read their HTML and grab their actual <title> tags to make your link titles descriptive.
  • External Link Attributes: All external links automatically receive target="_blank" (to open in a new tab) and rel="noopener noreferrer" (for security), with a helpful "(opens in new tab)" warning appended to the title.
  • Internal Link Accessibility: Titles are intentionally left off internal links. This follows modern web accessibility best practices by reducing auditory "noise" for screen reader users and avoiding tooltips that mobile/keyboard users cannot trigger.
  • Private Proxy Server: To bypass CORS restrictions and corporate firewalls securely, this tool routes all external requests through a dedicated Cloudflare Worker proxy, ensuring reliable connection to Whitman's XML feeds and external sites.
